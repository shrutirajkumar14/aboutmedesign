Introducing Shruti Rajkumar

Shruti Rajkumar is a journalist and a student at the Craig Nemwark Graduate School of Journalism at CUNY. Previously, they were a breaking news reporter for HuffPost. Rajkumar has a beat in disability reporting and has experience covering general assignment.

  1. Disabled Lives Are An Afterthought During School Shootings. But Why? - This article is about how disabled people are consistently left behind in emergency evacuations in schools across the U.S.
  2. Advocates Fear The Impact Of NYC’s Involuntary Hospitalization Plan - This article examines NYC's involuntary hospitalization plan of people with mental illnesses and how it is reflective of historic disability legislation from the 19th and 20th centuries.
  3. The ADA was a victory for the disabled community, but we need more. My life shows why - This article focuses on the progress of the U.S. disability rights movement and areas that need improvement.
